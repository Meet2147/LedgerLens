Why LedgerLens

Built for statements you can't upload

Financial data is sensitive. LedgerLens does the whole job locally — no account, no cloud, no compromise.

Every PDF is opened and parsed on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded — the app doesn't even send your data anywhere.

Multi-bank templates

Auto-detects statement layouts and extracts date, description, debit, credit and balance — reconciled to the penny.
